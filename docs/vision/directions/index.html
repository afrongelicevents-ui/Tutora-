Three design directions

Three very different looks for Tutora. Each shows the homepage, a parent's view of their child's progress and a student's plan for today at phone size (390 px), plus the homepage on a laptop. Choose one, or mix parts, and I will then design every page in that style.

All names, marks and dates are sample content to show the layout; the live site only ever shows real data. The photo is the one you gave me; each direction uses it differently. Nothing here changes the live site.

Direction A

Exercise book

Calm and trusted, like a good teacher's neatly kept exercise book: ruled lines, a red margin, handwritten-style order, no app chrome.

Colours (today's Tutora colours, used differently)

Fonts, buttons, cards

  • DM Serif Display for headings (already on the site, 25 KB); the phone's own font for text.
  • Buttons: square-cornered green blocks; secondary actions are words with an ochre underline.
  • No cards: content sits on ruled lines, numbered in the margin like a notebook.
  • Contrast: green on cream 7.7:1, clay 4.5:1, navy 10.3:1 (all pass AA).

Logo

Keeps today's wordmark exactly: serif "tutora" with the ochre "o".

Plain & precise

To compare

How they differ

A · Exercise bookClosest to today's Tutora. Feels like school in a good way: familiar to parents and teachers. Quiet, almost no colour blocks. Risk: may feel less "app-like" to teenagers.
B · SunriseMost friendly and energetic; best for students and younger children. Big tap targets. New colours and logo, so it is the biggest change from today. Adds 33 KB of font.
C · Plain & preciseMost serious and "trustworthy business". Fastest (no font download). Very clear for reports and the admin and tutor tools. Risk: can feel cold for children.
All threePass WCAG AA contrast, use one photo at most per screen, no gradients or shadows, work on cheap phones, and show only real data on the live site. You can mix, for example B's colours with C's layouts.
